A GOTCHA! PRODUCTIONS PROJECT

Talakbay is a thesis project developed by Gotcha! Productions that combines a Cinematic Instructional Video Series and a Structured Photowalk Program designed to help Grade 12 Arts and Design Track students develop their photography knowledge and skills through guided and experiential learning.

Our Collaborators

TaLakbay is developed with the collaboration of organizations that support its educational and creative context.

Multimedia Arts Council of the Philippines (MMACP)

The Multimedia Arts Council of the Philippines is a collaborating organization for TaLakbay. Its involvement connects the project with the wider multimedia arts community and supports creative collaboration in the field of multimedia arts.

Francisco E. Barzaga Integrated High School (FEBIHS)

Francisco E. Barzaga Integrated High School is TaLakbay's school collaborator. The project is designed for Grade 12 Arts and Design Track students, placing the program within a school-based learning context for photography and the application of selected elements and principles of organization in the arts.
